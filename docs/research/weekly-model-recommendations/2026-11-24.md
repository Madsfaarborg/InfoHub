### Coding evidence

Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index v2.1 compares Codex configurations under its benchmark protocols. Below are rounded scores on 132 FrontierCode-AA 2.0 repository implementation tasks, 66 Terminal-Bench 4.1 terminal tasks, and SWE-Atlas-QnA-AA repository questions. Reported cost combines metered usage with modeled provider rates and typical caching. Reported time and tokens are per attempted task across the index. The exact test date, dated model snapshot, configuration-specific retry counts and paired confidence intervals were not established from the comparison. Treat these as runner-level observations. [Results](https://artificialanalysis.ai/agents/coding-agents/comparisons/codex-vs-kimi-code-cli) · [Methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/coding-agents-benchmarking/)

| Codex configuration | FrontierCode | Terminal-Bench | Repo Q&A | USD/attempt | Minutes/attempt | Tokens/attempt |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GPT-6.2 Sol high | 38 | 54 | 51 | 0.76 | 10.6 | 2.96M |
| GPT-6.2 Sol xhigh | 40 | 58 | 51 | 0.90 | 12.6 | 3.26M |
| GPT-6.2 Astra high | 42 | 60 | 55 | 2.37 | 15.8 | 2.26M |
| GPT-6.2 Astra xhigh | 43 | 62 | 54 | 3.20 | 16.5 | 3.09M |

Sol xhigh cost about 18% more and took 19% longer than Sol high. Astra xhigh cost about 35% more than Astra high for a two-point terminal gain and a one-point decline in repo Q&A. The displayed gaps may fall within uncertainty. A lower token count for Astra high did not translate to lower modeled cost. Success-adjusted cost is unavailable because the displayed cost pools different task sets and comparable task-level spend and success counts were unavailable. This evidence leaves the small-patch value and source-based research hypotheses open.
